MY APPROACHI independently architected and implemented the core bill-run engine, from mapping through reporting. I used Cassandra-backed coordination to preserve stage progress across worker interruptions and make recovery part of the execution design.

05 / HOW I WORK

The details are the work.

Three things I pay attention to when building backend systems.

01

Failures are part of the design.

Retries, duplicate results, partial progress, and restarts change how a system behaves. I try to account for them upfront.

02

If it's hard to explain, I look again.

When a design needs too many special cases, I'd rather revisit the boundaries than add another exception.

03

Numbers need context.

I don't trust a performance number without knowing the workload, the environment, and what was actually measured.
