FEATURED INDEPENDENT PROJECTAI / PRODUCT ENGINEERING

Job Find Helper — AI Career Intelligence.

Job openings are scattered across company portals, while figuring out which roles actually fit a candidate takes its own effort. I built a platform to collect, synchronize, deduplicate, and classify openings, then rank them using more than keyword matching.

The recommendation approach combines semantic embeddings, skills, and recruitment-history signals with LLM-based explanations of possible skill gaps. Ranking quality is evaluated with Precision@K and NDCG@K.

PythonFastAPIReactEmbeddingsLLMsRanking evaluation
